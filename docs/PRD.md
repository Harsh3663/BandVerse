## 1. Vision

A world where **live musical talent is as easy to discover, evaluate, and hire as booking a ride or a hotel room**.

Today, the richness of a country's live-performance culture — wedding bands, Dhol Tasha troupes, banjo processions, folk ensembles, solo instrumentalists, DJs — lives entirely offline, scattered across word-of-mouth referrals, WhatsApp forwards, and Instagram DMs. BandVerse's vision is to become the **definitive digital home for live performance talent**: the place where a musician builds their professional identity, a band builds its business, and a customer finds — with confidence — exactly the right act for their moment.

Long-term, BandVerse aims to be to live performers what Spotify is to recorded music and Airbnb is to hospitality: the trusted layer of discovery, trust, and transaction for an entire creative economy that has never had one.

---

## 2. Mission

To build an **AI-powered ecosystem** that empowers every musician, band, and traditional performance group — regardless of size, genre, or technical sophistication — to:

1. Build a credible, professional online presence in minutes.
2. Get discovered by the right customers, nearby, at the right time.
3. Manage their craft as a business (bookings, pricing, team, calendar, reputation).
4. Get paid safely, transparently, and on time.

And to give every customer and event organizer the confidence to hire performers they've never met in person — through verified profiles, real reviews, rich media, and transparent pricing.

---

## 3. Problem Statement

Live performance talent — especially outside the top 1% of "celebrity" artists — operates in a **fundamentally broken, informal market**:

### For Artists, Bands & Traditional Groups
- **No professional online identity.** Most performers rely on a WhatsApp number and an Instagram reel. There is no dedicated place to showcase a full body of work, pricing, availability, or team.
- **Undiscoverable.** A Dhol Tasha group performing 60 events a year in Pune has almost zero visibility to a customer in the next locality who doesn't already know someone who knows them.
- **No pricing transparency or negotiation leverage.** Pricing is verbal, inconsistent, and often exploitative for newer artists.
- **No reputation system.** Great performers with 10 years of five-star word-of-mouth have no digital reputation asset. A single bad show and a great show look identical from the outside.
- **No business tooling.** Bands struggle to manage members, rehearsal schedules, practice locations, packages, and incoming leads — it's all spreadsheets, phone calls, and memory.
- **Traditional & folk art forms are economically invisible.** Dhol Tasha, Banjo, and Folk groups represent centuries of cultural heritage and real commercial demand (weddings, festivals, processions), yet have the *least* digital representation of any performer category — a massive, underserved niche.

### For Customers & Event Organizers
- **Discovery is inefficient and trust-deficient.** Finding "a good Dhol Tasha group near me, under a certain budget, available on a certain date" today requires 10+ phone calls and no way to compare.
- **No way to evaluate quality remotely.** Can't watch past performances, compare pricing side-by-side, or read verified reviews before committing money.
- **No unified booking or payment flow.** Everything is manual: cash negotiations, verbal confirmations, no digital paper trail, no cancellation protection.
- **No single platform spans *all* performer categories.** Existing booking sites (where they exist) focus narrowly on weddings or DJs — none unify solo artists, bands, traditional folk groups, and venues in one discovery layer.

### The Net Result
A **multi-billion-dollar informal live-performance economy** operating with 1990s-level tooling — no digital storefronts, no reputation infrastructure, no data, and massive friction on both sides of the transaction. This is the gap BandVerse fills.

## 5. Business Goals

1. **Establish category leadership** as the first platform to unify solo artists, bands, and traditional performance groups under one discovery and booking experience.
2. **Build marketplace liquidity** — reach a critical mass of supply (verified performer profiles) and demand (active searchers/bookers) in initial launch cities within 12–18 months.
3. **Create a durable revenue engine** through a blended model:
   - Commission on completed bookings (primary)
   - Subscription tiers for artists/bands (premium visibility, analytics, advanced tools)
   - Featured/sponsored placement in search & discovery
   - Value-added services (payment processing float, promoted "Build Your Own Band" packages)
4. **Digitize an underserved cultural economy** — become the platform of record for traditional performance groups (Dhol Tasha, Banjo, Folk), a category with no serious digital incumbent.
5. **Build a proprietary data & AI moat** — performance history, pricing benchmarks, geo-demand patterns, and taste graphs that compound over time and cannot be easily replicated.
6. **De-risk revenue from single-category dependence** by expanding into adjacent verticals (music teachers, event organizers, venues) once the core marketplace has liquidity.
7. **Prepare the platform for geographic and category expansion** (new cities, new performance categories, eventually international markets) without re-architecting the product.

## 7. User Stories

Grouped by capability area. Format: *As a [persona], I want to [do X], so that [benefit Y].*

### Discovery & Search
- As a **customer**, I want to search for artists/bands near my location, so that I can find performers who can realistically travel to my event.
- As a **customer**, I want to filter results by city, distance, price range, category, and rating, so that I can narrow down to relevant options quickly.
- As a **customer**, I want to view performers on a map alongside their practice locations, so that I understand where they operate from.
- As a **customer**, I want AI-recommended artists based on my event type, budget, and past searches, so that I don't have to manually sift through irrelevant profiles.

### Profiles & Portfolio
- As a **solo artist**, I want a professional profile with photos, videos, a performance timeline, and pricing, so that I can present myself credibly without a personal website.
- As a **band**, I want to manage members, roles, and internal permissions, so that the right people can update the right information.
- As a **band**, I want to showcase practice locations and schedules, so that potential joiners and customers understand our operating rhythm.
- As an **artist or band**, I want to link my social media, so that customers can verify authenticity and see broader work.

### Reviews, Ratings & Trust
- As a **customer**, I want to read verified reviews from past bookers, so that I can trust the quality of a performer before paying.
- As an **artist/band**, I want to respond to reviews, so that I can address feedback publicly and professionally.
- As an **admin**, I want to flag and moderate suspicious reviews or profiles, so that trust on the platform remains high.

### Booking & Calendar
- As a **customer**, I want to check an artist's real-time availability calendar, so that I don't waste time inquiring about already-booked dates.
- As an **artist/band**, I want to manage my calendar and block out unavailable dates, so that I avoid double-bookings.
- As a **customer**, I want to send a booking request with event details, so that the artist can confirm or negotiate before I pay.

### Chat & Communication
- As a **customer**, I want to chat directly with an artist/band within the platform, so that I can ask questions before booking without exposing my personal number upfront.
- As an **artist/band**, I want a unified inbox for all inbound leads, so that I never miss a potential booking.

### Payments
- As a **customer**, I want to pay securely online (including partial advance payments), so that I have a documented, safe transaction.
- As an **artist/band**, I want timely payouts with transparent fee breakdowns, so that I can trust the platform with my income.
- As an **admin**, I want visibility into all transactions and dispute cases, so that I can intervene when something goes wrong.

### Band Building & Talent Network
- As a **musician without a band**, I want to apply to join an existing band, so that I can find collaborative opportunities.
- As a **customer or organizer**, I want to "Build Your Own Band" by selecting individual musicians (e.g., a drummer + vocalist + guitarist), so that I can create a custom lineup for my event.
- As a **band leader**, I want to review and approve/reject join requests, so that I control who represents my band.

### Events & Live Performances
- As a **customer**, I want to see upcoming public live performances near me, so that I can discover talent by watching them perform before booking.
- As an **artist/band**, I want to publish upcoming performance events, so that I can build public visibility and audience engagement.

### Admin & Platform Operations
- As an **admin**, I want to verify new performer profiles before they go live, so that the marketplace maintains quality and trust.
- As an **admin**, I want a dashboard of platform health metrics (bookings, GMV, disputes, active users), so that I can monitor business performance.
- As an **admin**, I want tools to suspend or ban fraudulent accounts, so that the platform stays safe.

## 8. Functional Requirements

### 8.1 Authentication & Onboarding
- Email/password and Google OAuth sign-in (JWT-based session management).
- Role-based onboarding flows: Solo Artist, Band, Traditional Group, Restaurant/Venue, Event Organizer, Customer.
- Guided profile-completion wizard per role, with completeness scoring.

### 8.2 Profiles
- **Artist Profile:** bio, category/genre, photos, videos, performance timeline/history, pricing packages, availability calendar, social links, reviews/ratings summary.
- **Band Profile:** all of the above, plus member roster, role management (leader/member/admin), practice locations (map-pinned), practice schedule, group packages, join-request inbox.
- **Venue/Restaurant Profile:** location, capacity, booking calendar for recurring acts, preferred genres.
- Media uploads (photos/videos) via cloud storage with optimized delivery.

### 8.3 Discovery & Search
- Location-based ("nearby") search with radius/distance filtering.
- Filters: category, city, price range, rating, availability date, distance.
- Map-based browsing (performer locations, practice locations).
- Side-by-side comparison view for shortlisted performers.
- AI-driven recommendations based on user behavior, event type, and history.

### 8.4 Booking & Calendar
- Real-time availability calendar per artist/band.
- Booking request → negotiation/confirmation → payment flow.
- Booking status tracking (requested, confirmed, paid, completed, cancelled, disputed).
- Automated reminders/notifications for upcoming bookings.

### 8.5 Communication
- In-app real-time chat between customers and performers.
- Unified lead inbox for artists/bands.
- Notification system (push/email) for messages, booking updates, and reviews.

### 8.6 Payments
- Online payment processing (cards, UPI, netbanking) via Razorpay.
- Support for full and partial (advance) payments.
- Automated payout scheduling to performers, with transparent commission breakdown.
- Refund and cancellation-policy handling.

### 8.7 Reviews & Ratings
- Post-booking verified review and star-rating system (only completed bookings can review).
- Public response capability for performers.
- Review moderation queue for admin.

### 8.8 Band Building & Talent Network
- "Build Your Own Band" — customer/organizer selects individual musicians to assemble a custom lineup.
- "Apply to Join a Band" — solo musicians can browse open band positions and apply.
- Band leader approval workflow for join requests.

### 8.9 Events & Live Performances
- Public "Upcoming Live Performances" feed, filterable by location and date.
- Artists/bands can publish/promote performance events for audience discovery.

### 8.10 Admin Panel
- Profile verification and moderation queue.
- Dispute resolution workspace (booking/payment issues).
- Platform analytics dashboard (users, bookings, GMV, category breakdown).
- Content moderation tools (reviews, media, reported profiles).

### 8.11 AI & Recommendations
- Personalized artist/band recommendations (collaborative + content-based filtering, expanding to ML-driven ranking).
- Smart pricing insights for performers based on category/location benchmarks (future phase).

## 9. Non-Functional Requirements

| Category | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **Performance** | Sub-2-second page loads on core discovery flows; sub-500ms search filter response |
| **Scalability** | Architecture must support horizontal scaling from single-city launch to multi-city, multi-country expansion without re-platforming |
| **Availability** | 99.9% uptime target for core booking/payment flows |
| **Security** | Encrypted data at rest and in transit; secure JWT session handling; PCI-DSS-aligned payment handling via Razorpay; role-based access control |
| **Privacy & Compliance** | Compliance with India's DPDP Act (data privacy); secure handling of KYC data for payouts; clear data-retention policies |
| **Reliability of Media** | Fast, resilient photo/video delivery via CDN-backed cloud storage (Cloudinary), with graceful degradation on poor networks |
| **Usability** | Mobile-first responsive design; accessible to low-tech-literacy users (esp. traditional performance groups); WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility baseline |
| **Localization** | Multi-language support roadmap (Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, and other regional languages) given target demographic |
| **Observability** | Full application monitoring, error tracking, and analytics instrumentation from day one |
| **Maintainability** | Modular, well-documented codebase enabling incremental, sprint-based feature delivery per the team's build philosophy |
| **Data Integrity** | Idempotent payment and booking transactions; audit trails for all financial operations |

## 10. MVP Scope

The MVP is intentionally narrow: prove that **discovery → trust → booking** works end-to-end for a focused set of user types in a single launch city, before expanding.

### In Scope for MVP
- **Roles:** Solo Artist, Band, Dhol Tasha/Banjo/Folk Group (treated as a "Group" profile type), Customer, Admin. *(Restaurants & full Event Organizer tooling deferred — see Roadmap.)*
- **Core Profile:** photos, videos, bio, pricing, basic availability calendar, social links.
- **Discovery:** location-based search, city/category/price filters, map view of performers and practice locations.
- **Trust:** post-booking reviews & ratings, admin-verified profile badges.
- **Booking Flow:** booking request → chat negotiation → confirmation → online payment (full or advance) → completion.
- **Communication:** in-app chat.
- **Payments:** Razorpay integration for customer payments and basic performer payouts.
- **Admin:** manual profile verification, dispute intake, basic analytics dashboard.
- **Auth:** email/password + Google OAuth.

### Explicitly Out of Scope for MVP (Deferred)
- AI-driven recommendations (rule-based/manual curation initially instead).
- "Build Your Own Band" and "Apply to Join Band" workflows.
- Restaurant/venue recurring-booking tooling.
- Public "Upcoming Live Performances" event feed.
- Multi-city expansion (single launch city only).
- Native mobile apps (responsive web first).
- Advanced band team-management (multi-role permissions, complex package builders).

**Rationale:** This mirrors the team's stated build philosophy — one milestone, one module, one sprint at a time — and de-risks the business by validating the hardest problem first: *can we get customers to trust and pay for a performer discovered entirely online?* Everything else compounds on top of that proof point.

## 13. Risks

| Risk | Description | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Chicken-and-egg marketplace problem** | Customers won't come without supply; performers won't join without demand | Hyper-local, single-city launch with concierge-style manual onboarding of high-demand performer categories (e.g., popular Dhol Tasha groups) to seed initial supply |
| **Disintermediation** | Performers and customers may exchange contact info and transact off-platform after first connection | Value-added incentives to stay on-platform (payment protection, calendar tools, review-building, dispute resolution); commission structure that rewards repeat on-platform bookings |
| **Trust & safety failures** | Fake profiles, misrepresented media, no-shows, payment fraud | Mandatory profile verification, escrow-style payment holding, review authenticity checks, admin moderation tooling from day one |
| **Low digital literacy among traditional performer groups** | Dhol Tasha/Banjo/Folk groups may have limited comfort with digital tools | Simplified onboarding UX, assisted/manual onboarding support, local-language support |
| **Payment & regulatory compliance** | Handling online payments and payouts in India requires compliance with RBI/payment aggregator regulations | Partner with a compliant, licensed payment gateway (Razorpay) rather than building custom payment infrastructure; legal review before scaling payout volume |
| **Seasonality** | Wedding/festival-driven demand is highly seasonal | Diversify into corporate events, restaurants/venues, and recurring bookings to smooth demand |
| **Competitive response** | Existing booking platforms or new entrants could copy the model | Move fast to build network effects and reputation data (switching costs); focus on underserved traditional-category niche as a defensible wedge |
| **Content moderation at scale** | Media uploads, reviews, and chat all require moderation as volume grows | Combination of automated moderation tooling and human review workflows, scaled with growth |

## 14. Assumptions

- A meaningful share of target performers (solo artists, bands, traditional groups) have or can gain access to a smartphone and basic internet connectivity.
- Customers in target cities are increasingly comfortable paying online for services booked digitally (accelerated by UPI ubiquity in India).
- Traditional/folk performance categories represent significant, recurring commercial demand (weddings, festivals, religious/cultural events) that is currently unmet by any dedicated digital platform.
- A single-city, concentrated launch strategy will generate enough density for early network effects, rather than spreading thin across many cities.
- Performers are willing to pay a commission or subscription in exchange for meaningfully increased discoverability and reduced administrative burden.
- Google Maps-based location data is sufficiently accurate and available in target launch cities.
- Regulatory environment for online payments and marketplace commissions in India remains stable and Razorpay-compliant integration is sufficient for early-stage compliance needs.

## 15. Product Philosophy

1. **Trust before transaction.** Every feature decision is filtered through "does this increase or decrease trust between two strangers transacting for a live, in-person experience?"
2. **Discovery is the product.** The core value isn't a booking form — it's the moment a customer finds a performer they didn't know existed and falls in love with their work.
3. **Dignify the craft.** Traditional and folk performers are treated as first-class citizens of the platform, not an afterthought — their cultural and commercial value is central to BandVerse's identity, not a "diversity feature."
4. **Radical simplicity for the supply side.** A Dhol Tasha group leader with moderate tech comfort should be able to build a complete, professional profile in under 10 minutes.
5. **Build incrementally, validate constantly.** One milestone, one module, one sprint — no feature ships without a clear hypothesis about the user problem it solves.
6. **Mobile-first, always.** The overwhelming majority of both performers and customers will interact with BandVerse primarily via mobile devices.
7. **Data compounds trust.** Every completed booking, review, and interaction strengthens the platform's ability to recommend well and verify quality — this is the long-term moat.

## 16. Design Philosophy

BandVerse's design must feel like it belongs in the same conversation as **Apple, Spotify, Airbnb, Stripe, Linear, and Notion** — never like a "college project" or generic marketplace template.

- **Premium & elegant, not decorative.** Clean typography, generous whitespace, restrained color use, and purposeful motion (Framer Motion) — every animation should communicate state, not decorate the screen.
- **Content-forward, media-first.** Performers are visual and auditory by nature; the UI should foreground photos, videos, and performance media the way Spotify foregrounds album art — not bury it in generic form fields.
- **Fast and fluid.** Perceived performance matters as much as raw performance — skeleton loaders, optimistic UI updates, and instant filter feedback.
- **Consistent design system.** Built on shadcn/ui + Tailwind CSS primitives to guarantee visual and interaction consistency as the product scales across many screens and modules.
- **Map and location as a delight, not a utility.** Google Maps integration should feel exploratory and beautiful (custom map styling, smooth marker interactions), not like a dropped-in widget.
- **Accessible and inclusive.** High-contrast options, readable type scales, and simplified flows so that lower-tech-literacy users (a core part of our supply side) are never excluded from a "premium" experience.
- **Emotionally resonant.** This is a platform about music, celebration, and culture — the interface should feel warm and alive, not sterile and transactional like a generic B2B SaaS tool.

## 18. Why BandVerse Will Succeed

1. **A genuinely underserved, high-emotion, high-spend category.** Live performance bookings (especially weddings and festivals) are high-stakes, high-budget, infrequent purchases — exactly the kind of decision where trust, comparison, and rich media dramatically improve outcomes, yet no dedicated platform exists today.
2. **A defensible wedge nobody else is targeting.** Traditional and folk performance categories (Dhol Tasha, Banjo, Folk groups) are culturally significant, commercially active, and almost entirely absent from any existing digital platform — this is a genuine blue-ocean entry point that also builds enormous goodwill and defensibility before larger, generalist competitors notice the category.
3. **Two-sided value from day one.** Performers get a professional identity and business tooling they've never had access to; customers get trust and comparison they've never had access to — both sides are strictly better off, which drives organic word-of-mouth adoption on both sides of the marketplace.
4. **Timing tailwinds.** Rising smartphone penetration, UPI-driven comfort with digital payments, and the broader "creator economy" mindset shift make performers newly receptive to building an online business identity — a shift that wasn't true even five years ago.
5. **Compounding data moat.** Every booking, review, and search interaction improves BandVerse's recommendation and trust systems in ways that are extremely difficult for a fast-follower to replicate without the same density of real transaction data.
6. **Category-defining design ambition.** By refusing to look or feel like a "listings site" and instead building with the design rigor of Apple, Spotify, and Airbnb, BandVerse creates an immediate quality signal that differentiates it from every unorganized, WhatsApp-based alternative — the product itself becomes a trust signal.
7. **Disciplined, sprint-based execution philosophy.** Building one validated module at a time reduces wasted engineering effort, keeps the team focused on the riskiest assumptions first (trust + payment for a stranger-to-stranger live-performance transaction), and ensures the product that eventually scales is one that has been continuously validated with real users — not a big-bang launch built on assumptions.
